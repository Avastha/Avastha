The Story

Where it all began — Oscillation is the very first project Avastha ever started.

Dreamspace presents Oscillation, a new journey by Avastha. Built around deep hypnotic movement, golden psychedelic energy and a powerful rolling groove, it takes the listener into a frequency field where sound, rhythm and emotion move together like waves.

The track flows between tension and release, light and shadow, motion and stillness — a strong progressive psychedelic atmosphere made for deep dancefloor moments.

Massive basslines, hypnotic synths and powerful psychedelic drops: high-energy, driving psytrance tailored directly for the live dancefloor.

Turn up the volume and prepare for a massive frequency shift.
A pure sonic wave designed to move the body and open the mind.
